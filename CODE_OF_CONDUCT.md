# Code of conduct

Mirdain Print Hub is a community for practical, safety-conscious collaboration.
Be respectful, patient, and specific. Welcome people of different experience
levels and backgrounds, critique ideas rather than people, and make room for
others to participate.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misinformation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.
Do not pressure anyone to share private printer, business, network, or identity
information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation
that violates these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository
maintainers' GitHub profiles. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and routed to another
maintainer when possible.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially
representing the project elsewhere.
